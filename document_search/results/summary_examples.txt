TITLE: Mountain car problem (ai)

LLM SUMMARY (96 words):
The mountain car problem is a reinforcement learning task where an agent learns across two continuous variables, position and velocity, by choosing to drive left, drive right, or not use the engine. The agent receives a negative reward at each time step until reaching the goal. First appearing in Andrew Moore's 1990 PhD thesis, the problem requires extending discrete techniques using state space discretization, tile coding, or function approximation. Eligibility traces are also applied to speed up learning from delayed rewards. Many variations alter the problem's constants, reward functions, termination conditions, or continuous state space dimensions.

REFERENCE (Wikipedia lead, 96 words):
Mountain Car, a standard testing domain in Reinforcement learning, is a problem in which an under-powered car must drive up a steep hill. Since gravity is stronger than the car's engine, even at full throttle, the car cannot simply accelerate up the steep slope. The car is situated in a valley and must learn to leverage potential energy by driving up the opposite hill before the car is able to make it to the goal at the top of the rightmost hill. The domain has been used as a test bed in various reinforcement learning papers.

TITLE: Region connection calculus (ai)

LLM SUMMARY (94 words):
Region connection calculus is governed by two axioms: any region connects with itself, and if region x connects with y, y connects with x. The RCC8 calculus is intended for reasoning about spatial configurations using constraint networks, which can be refined through its composition table, the path-consistency algorithm, and consistent singleton-labelings. Other versions include RCC5, which uses five basic relations and ignores whether regions touch, and RCC23, which allows reasoning about convexity. Additionally, RCC8 is partially implemented in GeoSPARQL, while tools like the GQR reasoner and qualreas Python framework provide qualitative spatial reasoning implementations.

REFERENCE (Wikipedia lead, 94 words):
The region connection calculus (RCC) is intended to serve for qualitative spatial representation and reasoning. RCC abstractly describes regions (in Euclidean space, or in a topological space) by their possible relations to each other. RCC8 consists of 8 basic relations that are possible between two regions: 
disconnected (DC)
externally connected (EC)
equal (EQ)
partially overlapping (PO)
tangential proper part (TPP)
tangential proper part inverse (TPPi)
non-tangential proper part (NTPP)
non-tangential proper part inverse (NTPPi)
From these basic relations, combinations can be built. For example, proper part (PP) is the union of TPP and NTPP.

TITLE: Reasoning model (ai)

LLM SUMMARY (61 words):
Reasoning language models, developed by creators like OpenAI, DeepSeek, and Moonshot AI, tackle complex tasks through scaled computing, knowledge distillation, and agent swarms. These systems are trained through supervised fine-tuning on step-by-step reasoning traces, often generated automatically with rejection sampling. Furthermore, reinforcement learning applies policy-gradient methods such as PPO alongside outcome reward models to guide models and reward correct final answers.

REFERENCE (Wikipedia lead, 64 words):
Reasoning language models (RLMs) or large reasoning models (LRMs) are large language models that are trained to solve tasks that require several steps of reasoning. They tend to do better on logic, math, and programming tasks than standard LLMs, can revisit and revise earlier steps, make use of extra computations, and enhance performance when increasing the number of parameters, training examples, and training computations.

Later episodes honored former cast members Bill Paxton and Powers Boothe with onscreen tributes following their deaths in 2017. Moving to a 10 pm timeslot enabled the series to adopt a darker, mature tone labeled "Agents of S.H.I.E.L.D. After Dark," which allowed for edgier themes, partial nudity, and increased violence. The mystical Darkhold acted as the primary connector across these arcs, transitioning the storyline from Ghost Rider's magic to advanced science and the Framework.
